# Code of Conduct

MixConsole adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
by reference, including its standards, scope, and enforcement guidelines.

Treat contributors with respect, welcome different backgrounds and experience
levels, and keep feedback constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.

The project maintainer, [roop37](https://github.com/roop37), is responsible for
enforcement. Use a private contact method listed on that profile to report an
incident. If none is available, request a private contact channel in an issue
without posting sensitive details. Reports will be handled discreetly and fairly;
responses can include warnings, temporary restrictions, or removal from the community.
